One short follow-up from your last answers — a single question, and it's about how things already
work rather than a decision you need to make.

## 1. Whose phone is it?

When we asked about the end of the day, you said the phone *"stays at the shop and vehicle."* We want
to make sure we've read that the right way, because it can mean two different things:

**(a) Each driver has their own phone.** It lives in their van during the day and comes back to the
shop at night — but nobody else uses it.

**(b) Each van has a phone.** It lives in the van, and whoever is driving that van today uses it.
Tomorrow it might be somebody else.

*Why we're asking:* it changes how the driver starts their day.

Under **(a)**, the phone knows who the driver is permanently. They never sign in — they just open the
app in the morning and tap "Start work."

Under **(b)**, the phone can't know who's holding it, so the driver has to sign in as themselves each
morning — and once they've done that, the separate "Start work" tap is redundant. Signing in *is*
starting work, and signing out at the end of the day is finishing. One step instead of two, but it's a
step they can't skip.

There's a second reason it matters. Your office sees each driver's position on a map while they're out
delivering. Under **(a)** that name is always right. Under **(b)** it's right only if whoever took the
van that morning remembered to sign in as themselves — otherwise the map shows yesterday's driver.

With two drivers and two vans these probably amount to the same thing today. They come apart the first
time a driver takes the other van.

*What we'd suggest:* nothing — this one is a fact about your shop, not a choice. We just need to know
which it is. If the honest answer is "it's (a) now, but I wouldn't want to promise that," tell us that
too; it's a useful answer.

Thanks.
